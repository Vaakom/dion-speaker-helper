# Privacy

The extension observes DION participant labels, active-speaker indicators, meeting page titles, timestamps, tab/document identifiers and hashed meeting routes. The local Native Messaging helper saves these on the same computer. The extension has no audio recorder, analytics or upload endpoint.

Automatic journaling runs while an observable DION meeting is open. Close the meeting or disable the extension to stop. Local files remain until you remove them. Your storage location may be synchronized by other software.

The diagnostic button is optional and saves bounded DOM fragments and styles; review and redact diagnostic files before sharing. Hashed URLs are identifiers, not a guarantee of anonymization.
